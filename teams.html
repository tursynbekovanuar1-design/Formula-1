Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Constructor's Championship?

The World Constructors' Championship (WCC) is awarded by the FIA to the most successful F1 constructor over the course of the season, based on points accumulated from race finishes.

How are race points allocated?

Points are awarded to the top 10 finishing drivers at each Grand Prix, ranging from 25 points for 1st place down to 1 point for 10th place, plus an extra point for the fastest lap.
